Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ current_year }}

What we collect
  • Account data: username, email, display name, hashed password, and your tier.
  • Profile content: headline, bio, links you publish, and avatar images you upload (stored on our own servers, never sold).
  • Usage data: aggregate click counts on your links. We do not sell account or profile data.
Cookies, analytics and advertising

We use a small number of cookies. Optional ones load only according to your choice (visitors outside the United States are asked first; visitors in the United States can opt out at any time). Use Cookie Settings / Your Privacy Choices to change your mind. We honor Global Privacy Control signals.

How we use data

To operate the service, secure it against abuse, notify you about broken links or account events through your inbox, and provide support. Admins may impersonate an account temporarily for support purposes; every such session is logged.

Sharing

We share data only with infrastructure providers needed to run the site (hosting, database) and never with data brokers. Law enforcement requests are honored where legally required.

Your rights

You can view and edit most of your data in the Dashboard at any time. To export or permanently delete your account and all uploaded files, contact us via the contact form; we respond within 30 days.

Security

Passwords are salted and hashed, uploads are re-encoded to strip metadata (including EXIF location), and production traffic should be served over HTTPS.

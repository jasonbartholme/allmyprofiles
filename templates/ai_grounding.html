What this product does

{{ site_name }} is a link-in-bio and personal-brand hosting platform. A user claims one unique username (for example {{ base }}/u/yourname) and receives a fast, mobile-first page that lists every profile, channel, storefront, article, or destination they own — in one place. The product is built so those pages rank well on Google and parse cleanly for AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude.

Who it is for

  • Creators, freelancers, developers, and professionals unifying their digital footprint.
  • Small businesses that want one branded link across social bios, print, and email signatures.
  • Anyone who needs a single, permanent URL that points to everything they own.

Key differentiators

  • SEO & AI-search optimization: every public profile embeds schema.org ProfilePage + Person JSON-LD with sameAs references to the owner's external profiles, plus semantic HTML blocks designed for LLM extraction.
  • Canonical URLs: public profiles resolve at /u/<username>; bare /<username> aliases exist for convenience.
  • Machine-readable feeds: each profile publishes an XML link feed at /u/<username>/links.xml.
  • Built-in traffic intelligence: clean click tracking per link, view counts, and an audience dashboard.
  • Modular customization: drag-and-drop link ordering, themes, custom backgrounds, and dynamic embeds (YouTube, Spotify, GitHub).

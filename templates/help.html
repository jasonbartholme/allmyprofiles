Help Center

Getting started
  1. Create your account — pick the username that becomes your public link (/u/yourname).
  2. Add your links — from the Dashboard, give each link a title and URL. Free plans include {{ free_max|default(3) }} active links.
  3. Customize — upload an avatar, set a display name, headline, and bio. Paid plans unlock themes and styling.
  4. Reorder & hide — drag links into priority order, or toggle them off without deleting them.
  5. Share it everywhere — put your one link in every social bio.
Analytics

Every click on your public page is counted on the link card in your Dashboard. Higher tiers add standard and advanced analytics, plus Google Analytics / Tag Manager and Meta pixel integrations.

Search & AI visibility

Public profiles publish JSON-LD (schema.org Person) markup and Open Graph tags automatically, so Google snippets are richer and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity) can attribute your links to you correctly.
